CASE STUDY 02 · SERVICE DELIVERY · CAPGEMINI

The calm layer
in the escalation.

Technical ownership and clear coordination across APAC support calls, executive stakeholders, and ITSM workflows.

EnvironmentAPAC enterprise supportFocusServiceNow · EscalationsDatesFeb 2025 — May 2025

The situation

High-priority incidents needed more than a ticket update.

Cross-regional support depends on more than technical troubleshooting. It needs someone who can understand the issue, keep the right people aligned, and translate operational detail into a clear next decision for stakeholders.

My contribution

Keep the technical path and the stakeholder path moving together.

  • Deputized for the APAC IT manager during service-delivery work.
  • Led cross-regional support calls with counterparts in India, the Middle East, and Egypt.
  • Liaised with Directors and VPs on escalations and service delivery.
  • Delivered end-to-end desktop support for enterprise clients, including C-level executives.
  • Documented technical procedures and troubleshooting solutions for future reference.

Operational layer

Make the handoff visible, actionable, and repeatable.

ServiceNowMicrosoft 365Entra IDIntuneAPAC coordinationExecutive support

The same operating mindset carried into onboarding and offboarding across APAC: same-day account removal for leavers, equipment readiness for new joiners, and a documented trail for the team.

Outcome

Technical detail translated into decisions.

The work strengthened service continuity across regional teams while giving senior stakeholders a clear point of ownership during high-pressure incidents and executive support moments.
