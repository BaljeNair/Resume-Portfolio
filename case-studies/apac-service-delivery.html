CASE STUDY 02 · SERVICE DELIVERY · CAPGEMINI

The calm layer
in the escalation.

Singapore office support at 80–100 users and 30–40 ServiceNow tickets per week, alongside APAC coordination and C-level escalations.

RoleConsultant · Feb–May 2025Volume80–100 users · 30–40 tickets/weekFocusServiceNow · Microsoft 365

The situation

Busy Singapore support needed steady ownership.

As a Consultant at Capgemini from February to May 2025, I supported about 80–100 users in the Singapore office, handling roughly 30–40 tickets a week in ServiceNow alongside Microsoft 365 support.

My contribution

Keep volume moving without losing the human thread.

  • Handled day-to-day desktop and Microsoft 365 support through ServiceNow.
  • Deputised for the APAC IT Manager when coordination needed a clear owner.
  • Led cross-regional support calls with teams across the region.
  • Handled C-level communications and escalations when incidents needed a steady hand.

Tools and decisions

Translate ticket volume into clear next actions.

ServiceNowMicrosoft 365Active DirectoryEntra IDAPAC coordinationC-level support

The role combined a measurable support workload with regional coordination. The value was not only resolving tickets; it was keeping communication calm and making sure the right stakeholders knew what would happen next.

What it shows

Volume plus calm under pressure.

Steady ticket throughput for a busy Singapore office, combined with the trust to step up for APAC coordination and senior stakeholders.
