CASE STUDY 03 · AV / EXECUTIVE READINESS · BP + CAPGEMINI

When the room
cannot fail.

Daily Teams and Zoom Rooms checks, plus responsive on-site support for high-profile leadership events where the room has to be ready before the first person walks in.

EnvironmentTeams Rooms · Zoom RoomsEquipmentPolycom · LogitechFocusExecutive and leadership events

The situation

Small room failures become visible very quickly.

At British Petroleum and Capgemini, meeting rooms and executive sessions depended on reliable video, audio, displays, and room controls. A missed check can become a visible interruption when a leadership meeting or townhall is already underway.

My contribution

Make readiness a daily habit, not a last-minute scramble.

  • Ran daily Teams / Zoom Rooms checks before scheduled meetings.
  • Checked room hardware, displays, audio paths, cameras, and collaboration controls.
  • Worked with Polycom and Logitech equipment across meeting spaces.
  • Provided responsive on-site delivery and support for high-profile leadership events.

Tools and decisions

Verify the path people will actually use.

Microsoft Teams RoomsZoom RoomsPolycomLogitechOn-site support

The practical approach was simple: check the complete meeting path rather than a single device. Confirm the room can connect, display, hear, and be heard; then stay ready to respond when the event matters.

What it shows

Reliable support includes the moments people remember.

Daily operational discipline and calm on-site response help leadership events feel effortless, even when the technology underneath is complex.
