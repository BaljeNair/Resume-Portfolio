CASE STUDY 04 · IDENTITY + DEVICE MANAGEMENT · APAC

Make onboarding
feel invisible.

A practical look at keeping joiners ready on day one and leavers closed out cleanly across identity, devices, approvals, and evidence.

WorkflowOnboarding · offboardingSystemsServiceNow · Entra ID · IntuneControlsMFA · BitLocker · equipment

The situation

A lifecycle handoff is more than an account ticket.

Across APAC, new joiners needed usable equipment and access on day one, while leavers needed access removed and equipment accounted for without delay. The operational challenge was connecting the identity, device, approval, and service-management steps instead of treating each as an isolated request.

My contribution

Carry the workflow from request to closure.

  • Managed end-to-end onboarding and offboarding workflows through ServiceNow ITSM.
  • Coordinated account creation and removal across Entra ID / Active Directory.
  • Prepared and tracked devices through Intune, BitLocker, and endpoint-management controls.
  • Kept MFA, equipment readiness, approvals, and closure evidence visible to the next owner.

Tools and decisions

Make the secure path easy to follow.

ServiceNow ITSMEntra IDIntuneMFABitLockerAPAC operations

The useful handoff is the one another engineer can pick up without reconstructing the story. Clear ownership, status, and evidence reduce the chance that an account remains active, a device is missing, or a new starter arrives without the tools to work.

What it shows

Good service delivery connects people, systems, and proof.

This work demonstrates an operational mindset: account lifecycle tasks are not finished when a ticket is moved; they are finished when access, equipment, approvals, and evidence are aligned.
