Operations Lab 02 · Case study

Make the handoff visible.

A browser-based simulator that makes joiner, mover, and leaver controls visible without connecting to a real directory, ITSM platform, or employer environment.

JML WORKFLOWPERSONAL / SYNTHETIC
CONTROLLED FLOW

Identity, device, approval, and evidence treated as one operational handoff.

Problem

Identity lifecycle work is easy to describe poorly.

A joiner needs access and equipment ready on day one. A mover needs access changed with the role. A leaver needs access removed and equipment recovered at the right time. A portfolio project needed to show that sequence without implying a production integration.

Approach

Three controlled workflow states.

JoinerConfirm manager and start date, create identity access, prepare an encrypted device, validate MFA and collaboration access, then record the handoff.
MoverConfirm the new role and effective date, review old access, apply the new access profile, reconcile device and software needs, and record approval evidence.
LeaverConfirm the last working day, disable identity and active sessions, remove access, recover the device, and record closure evidence.

Design decisions

Make the next owner’s responsibilities visible.

Each event uses the same interaction pattern: choose a lifecycle event, complete its checklist, watch progress update, and inspect the audit trail. Switching event type resets the checklist so one person’s state does not carry into another lifecycle event.

Outcome

ITSM and identity-management thinking, made inspectable.

A recruiter can switch between joiner, mover, and leaver scenarios, complete controls, and see how the handoff becomes more explicit as the workflow progresses. The simulator does not automate Entra ID, Intune, ServiceNow, or directory actions; it demonstrates the reasoning and interface design behind a safer operational workflow.

Personal project / synthetic data. All names, dates, assignments, and workflow records are fictional. The simulator does not connect to or modify any real identity, device, ticketing, or application system.
